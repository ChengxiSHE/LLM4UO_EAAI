## Defensible novelty statement

The paper does not claim priority for skills, typed contracts, validation-gated
updates, executable knowledge, applicability filters, versioned libraries, or
paired skill evaluation. Its narrower contribution is the optimization-
modeling-specific composition of:

1. typed formulation semantics and exact resource obligations;
2. a trusted compiler and fail-closed public-input router;
3. content-addressed lineage with parent, rollback, and accepted/rejected
   provenance;
4. frozen solver-facing formulation endpoints with full-denominator correction
   and regression accounting; and
5. a rights-safe public integrity layer separated from controlled endpoint
   rescoring.

This composition is demonstrated as an audited systems case study. A stronger
claim that it is superior to the closest integrated system requires a faithful
common-task implementation or released-code comparison and is not made here.
