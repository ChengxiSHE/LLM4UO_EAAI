# FormulationBench same-knowledge prospective experiment

The full prospective plan was frozen only after the synthetic capacity gate completed 8/8 requests without retry, failure, or restart duplication. It covers all 89 official FormulationBench reformulation pairs and both prespecified arms, for 178 logical requests. No pair was selected or removed using a label, proof, solution, provider response, or downstream outcome.

For every pair, the source custodian builds one canonical typed fact graph from the answer-free inference projection. `Flat-K` serializes that graph as a flat inventory. `PCK-K` places the identical facts under six typed obligations: parameter compatibility, forward feasibility, backward feasibility, integrality and bounds, index and quantifier coverage, and objective preservation. The frozen audit verifies exactly two arms and one identical fact-hash multiset for every one of the 89 opaque pair identities. Thus the experiment isolates representation and routing of knowledge while holding semantic knowledge content and generic guidance fixed.

The endpoint is AntChat `DeepSeek-V4-Flash`. Both arms use temperature zero, a 1,024-token completion cap, five concurrent requests, and at most three ledgered attempts per request fingerprint. Requests that exhaust retryable transport attempts remain in the full denominator as incorrect predictions; authentication, model, budget, ledger, and sustained transport failures halt globally. Checkpoints contain private typed judgments and are reused on restart. Labels remain unavailable to the runner and are opened only after all 178 cells are terminal.

The primary endpoint is full-denominator equivalence accuracy against the benchmark's public answer. The prespecified comparison is `PCK-K - Flat-K`; success requires higher `PCK-K` accuracy and a one-sided exact McNemar p-value at most 0.05. Missing, invalid, or infrastructure-unavailable predictions count as incorrect. No subset is used for the primary inference. Wilson 95% intervals, paired corrections and regressions, and problem-level descriptive subgroups are reported after the primary result.

The plan audit passed 12/12 checks: 89 pairs, 178 cells, two arms per pair, fact equality per pair, capacity-gate binding, concurrency five, 1,024 completion tokens, three attempts, deterministic plan and private request reconstruction, no evaluator path, and no answer-bearing public plan field. Freezing used zero API, model, solver, Lean, native, or VM calls and did not open evaluator custody. A positive result would identify a same-fact representation effect on this fixed FormulationBench endpoint; it would not establish general benchmark superiority or reproduce FormulationBench's original method.
