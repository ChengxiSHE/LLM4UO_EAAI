# FormulationBench same-knowledge result

The prospective comparison is complete on all 89 official pairs and its primary gate failed. `Flat-K` obtained 40/89 correct (44.94%; Wilson 95% interval 35.03--55.27%), while `PCK-K` obtained 36/89 (40.45%; 30.85--50.84%). The paired table contains 7 `PCK-K` corrections, 11 regressions, 29 pairs correct under both arms, and 42 incorrect under both. The prespecified one-sided exact McNemar p-value is 0.8811. The result therefore does not support a beneficial same-fact representation effect.

Execution was operationally complete and independently reconstructed. All 178 cells reached a terminal checkpoint in 178 physical sends, with zero retry, zero network or environment failure, zero global halt, and zero new sends on restart. There were 145 valid typed judgments and 33 method failures. Fifteen failures were provider completions that did not end with `stop` under the frozen 1,024-token cap; eighteen more failed the strict typed response contract. These are method failures, remain in the full denominator, and must not be described as infrastructure unavailable.

The post-hoc diagnostic identifies a concrete mechanism problem. `Flat-K` produced 77 valid judgments and 12 method failures; `PCK-K` produced 68 valid judgments and 21 method failures. Conditional on a valid judgment, the accuracies were 40/77 (51.95%) and 36/68 (52.94%), respectively. This valid-only comparison is selective and cannot rescue the failed primary endpoint, but it indicates that the large mandatory evidence object erased any small decision-quality benefit by increasing contract failure. The next design should make the decision channel extremely short, move evidence to an optional or separately budgeted channel, and route only the fact subset relevant to each obligation.

This 89-pair endpoint is now development evidence because its labels and outcomes have been observed. Any revised method may be studied here for diagnosis, but a new confirmatory accuracy claim must use an independently frozen endpoint or new source lineage. The negative result, frozen plan, full-denominator score, terminal reconstruction audit, and diagnostic will remain in the evidence chain rather than being replaced.
