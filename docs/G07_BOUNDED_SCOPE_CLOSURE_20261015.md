# G07 bounded-scope closure

Status: **closed with boundary; no new dispatch**.

## Decision

G07 asked whether the current method, controls, and runnable external methods
were compared under one fresh common protocol. The completed common-38 study
satisfies that requirement. On the same 38 body-isolated RCPSP/VRPTW
identities and fixed endpoint, current/Base/BIND-round-1/BIND-final/CoE-safe
score `30/0/13/13/4`. Current versus those four comparators yields paired
corrections/regressions of `30/0`, `18/1`, `18/1`, and `26/0`; all four
one-sided exact contrasts reject after Holm correction.

This closes G07 only as a fixed-endpoint comparison over two families from one
source lineage. It does not establish broad benchmark superiority, equalize
library-construction cost or provider tokens, convert BIND final into an
answer-blind condition, or supply a valid one-call CoE terminal.

## Why no second-source dispatch was added

The second-source requirement arose from the stronger broad-superiority claim,
not from the stated G07 completion criterion. The two available candidates do
not support a valid immediate dispatch:

- **DCP-Bench:** the frozen 135-identity selection study produced 41/135 for
  ordinary compilation, 44/135 for recovery, and 45/135 for feedback. Recovery
  corrected three ordinary failures without an observed regression, but its
  primary comparison against feedback was 6 corrections and 7 regressions.
  The prespecified promotion gate therefore failed. The reserved 219-identity
  confirmation partition remains sealed. Opening it now would convert a failed
  development gate into a post-outcome confirmation attempt.
- **MIPLIB-NL:** the pinned source remains a metadata/readiness inventory. It
  lacks a frozen scoreable endpoint, resolved semantic lineage, complete
  public-data binding, and fixed resource/reference contract. Dispatching it
  would create an outcome before the estimand and denominator were valid.

The scientifically conservative decision is therefore to preserve both
boundaries. A second source should be added only through a separately frozen,
score-ready protocol and is required before any paper claim of broad benchmark
superiority.

## Claim that may be used

> On a fresh fixed 38-identity RCPSP/VRPTW endpoint, proof-carrying knowledge
> compilation substantially outperforms locally rerun Base, BIND, and CoE-safe
> conditions under their disclosed information and execution contracts.

The paper must immediately qualify this as a two-family, single-source-lineage
result with unequal native trajectories and unequal construction cost.

## Execution and privacy

This closure is a read-only synthesis of already frozen, body-free aggregate
evidence. It made no API, model, solver, native-evaluator, or VM call; opened no
sealed confirmation set; and read no problem body, request or response body,
evaluator-only answer, or credential.
