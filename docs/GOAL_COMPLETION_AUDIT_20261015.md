## Decision

The project now has a coherent method, two strong bounded endpoint studies, a
fresh common-38 comparison against locally rerun Base, BIND and CoE-safe,
exact finite mechanism censuses, explicit theory boundaries and typed
RCPSP/VRPTW compilers. It is not yet a complete top-conference package because
the corrected immutable candidate has not yet been published, archived or
audited after publication. D1--D8 are author-approved: scoped Apache-2.0 and CC
BY 4.0 licenses, NOTICE, verified author metadata, availability and AI-use
disclosures, and history-free publication authorization are bound. D5 is
implemented by one durable no-clobber publisher serving nine source-hash-bound
adapters. The final provider audit also removed an untracked private-runtime
dependency from the public preflight. The language-model acquisition canary
remains a reported negative result; bounded active acquisition and all four
finite component effects are closed only in their declared grammars. The live
repository URL, immutable release identity, DOI and post-publication independent
audit remain open.

## External-baseline interpretation

- **BIND:** locally rerun and usable as two disclosed rows. Its answer-blind
  first round and answer-bearing final must never be merged.
- **CoE:** locally rerun from official code. The author-exact system-plus-safe-
  adapter result is valid, while unsupported generated programs prevent a claim
  about unrestricted native CoE ability.
- **OptiTrust:** the official-artifact audit is complete; official reproduction
  remains unavailable. Published scores cannot be substituted.
- **Current method:** the TSP and OR-Debug rows cannot be numerically inserted
  into the RCPSP/VRPTW external table because they use different sources and
  endpoints. The fresh common-38 protocol now supplies a matched current row
  and locally rerun Base/BIND/CoE-safe rows; its inference budgets and
  construction costs remain unequal and its two families share one source
  lineage.
