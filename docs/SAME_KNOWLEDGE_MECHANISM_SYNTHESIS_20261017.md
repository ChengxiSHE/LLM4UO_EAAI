# Same-knowledge mechanism synthesis

Status: **the current representation-only mechanism claim is not supported;
the complete-system benchmark results remain valid within their stated
boundaries**.

The strict FormulationBench comparison held 4,935 fact identities and six
generic checks fixed across all 89 pairs. Flat-K scored 40/89 and PCK-K scored
36/89, with 7 PCK-K corrections, 11 regressions, and one-sided exact McNemar
`p=0.8811`. PCK-K also produced more method failures under the mandatory
evidence contract, 21 versus 12. This prospective study therefore does not
support an accuracy benefit from proof-carrying organization when fact content
is fixed.

NLEquiv-150 separates two related questions on an independent source lineage.
Reorganizing the same facts into structured obligations left aggregate
accuracy unchanged at 124/150, with 13 corrections and 13 regressions relative
to the flat arm. Adding routed generic nonlinear-equivalence skill cards raised
accuracy to 128/150, but 13 corrections and 9 regressions gave `p=0.2617`, so
the prespecified gate also failed. NLEquiv is an equivalence-classification
endpoint rather than direct solver-code formulation accuracy.

Together, the two studies rule out the intended strong story that proof-
carrying representation or fact organization alone has been shown to improve
accuracy. They do not invalidate the frozen TSP, OR-Debug, or common-38 system-
level results, which evaluate complete benchmark-specific knowledge systems.
The defensible contribution is narrower: benchmark knowledge can be compiled
into a typed, auditable, fail-closed runtime with explicit routing, resource,
lineage, and promotion boundaries, while endpoint benefit depends on the
knowledge content and on keeping representation overhead below the model's
decision budget.

The next method version must follow five constraints derived from these
failures: keep the decision channel short; generate proof evidence separately
or optionally; retrieve only obligation-relevant facts; add necessary-condition
and counterexample-witness skills; and test the frozen revision on a new
independent direct-modeling endpoint. Both current endpoints are opened
development evidence and cannot confirm the revision.

Machine-readable evidence is
`experiments/same_knowledge_mechanism_synthesis_20261017.json`. It contains no
problem body, request or response body, per-item label or prediction, or
credential.
