## Execution order

### Phase A. Make historical publication durable — completed

The approved architecture is one shared atomic publisher with nine thin
adapters. It satisfies the following frozen contract:

1. preserve every frozen scientific field, byte serialization, path and hash;
2. reserve terminal ownership exclusively before publication;
3. write through a unique temporary path, then flush the file and containing
   directory before an atomic no-clobber commit;
4. treat an existing byte-identical terminal artifact as idempotent success and
   any mismatch, symlink, path escape or race as a closed failure;
5. define crash recovery without a time-to-live assumption; and
6. treat paired JSON and Markdown outputs as one recoverable transaction.

Required validation includes byte-equivalence tests, two-process race tests,
crash injection at every durability boundary, path and symlink rejection,
directory-durability checks and a complete Layer-A reconstruction.

## Current scientific decision

The language-model feedback canary is a completed negative result and must not
be retuned on the same eight pairs. The separate balanced-witness census is a
positive exact result inside an authored finite grammar. The paper may claim
bounded active acquisition, but not autonomous natural-language skill
discovery.

The fresh common-38 experiment is complete. It supports the observed large
fixed-endpoint advantage over the locally rerun rows on two larger families
from one source lineage. It does not support broad benchmark superiority,
equal-cost superiority or an unrestricted leaderboard claim.

The semantic, resource, routing and compactness effects are identified only in
their declared complete finite populations. No sampling p-value or natural
proposer-distribution claim should be attached to those censuses.

## Stop and promotion rules

- Any semantic or declared resource violation blocks promotion regardless of
  endpoint score.
- Confirmation cannot be opened unless the frozen promotion gate passes.
- Zero observed regressions on an exposed cohort is not a future non-regression
  theorem.
- Infrastructure unavailability, method failure, abstention and incorrect
  output remain distinct and stay in the declared denominator.
- BIND answer-bearing final remains separate from answer-blind comparison.
- CoE-safe scores measure the disclosed system plus bounded adapter, not
  unrestricted native CoE ability.
- OptiTrust artifact unavailability is not a zero score.
- The TSP, OR-Debug, common-38 and finite-census endpoints are frozen and must
  not be retuned for a better paper narrative.

## Paper narrative after Scheme A

The defensible paper story is now:

1. benchmark-derived modeling knowledge is useful but difficult to verify,
   route and update safely;
2. Benchmark-Grounded Proof-Carrying Knowledge Compilation turns that knowledge
   into typed, versioned programs with explicit semantic and resource
   obligations;
3. authored TSP and OR-Debug skills show large prospective full-denominator
   gains on two sources and task types;
4. a fresh common-38 protocol places the current method, Base, BIND and CoE-safe
   under one frozen endpoint and shows a large bounded advantage;
5. exact finite censuses identify balanced acquisition plus semantic, resource,
   routing and compactness effects at their valid experimental units;
6. the mixed library preserves all 2,985 direct-family outputs and fails closed
   on zero or multiple matches; and
7. the negative language-model canary, one-source-lineage common cohort,
   authored grammars and release boundary delimit what the paper cannot claim.

The research goal remains active until the licensed rights-safe public artifact,
author and archival fields, and final independent audit are complete. Stronger
natural-language acquisition, natural library evolution, learned routing and
second-source broad-superiority studies are valuable extensions, not hidden
requirements for the bounded claims already reported.
