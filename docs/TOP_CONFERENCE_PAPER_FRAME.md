## Authoritative current frame — 2026-10-14

This section supersedes later passages that still describe TSP or OR-Debug as

**One-sentence argument.** We show that optimization-modeling knowledge can be
represented as versioned, proof-carrying skill programs rather than prompt
text: benchmark failures guide typed rule construction, a fixed verifier and
compiler enforce declared semantic and resource obligations, a public-only
router abstains on ambiguity, and a frozen grouped endpoint determines
promotion.  The method improves strong static controls on prospective TSP and
OR-Debug confirmation splits, while its guarantees remain conditional on the
typed contract and evaluator.

The canonical method name is **Benchmark-Grounded Proof-Carrying Skill
Evolution**.  Its scientific object is the versioned skill library
`L_t=(K_t,rho_t,V,E,ledger_t)`, not the Codex goal loop.  `K_t` contains
content-addressed typed skills, `rho_t` is a public-only selective router, `V`
is an immutable verifier, `E` is the frozen benchmark endpoint, and `ledger_t`
records accepted and rejected lineage.  The proposer may be human, finite
search, or an LLM; proposer autonomy is an experimental factor rather than a
correctness premise.
Canonical manuscript vocabulary and notation are fixed in the
[paper terminology ledger](PAPER_TERMINOLOGY_LEDGER_20261014.md).

### Main contributions supported by current evidence

1. **Executable knowledge-acquisition methodology.** A skill is a tuple of an
   applicability guard, typed evidence extractor, transformation, verifier,
   resource checker and provenance.  Finite counterexample-guided search,
   proof-carrying compilation, selective routing and risk-controlled promotion
   define a reproducible library transition rather than informal prompt tuning.
2. **Conditional reliability framework.** Finite termination and correctness
   on enumerated verification domains are bounded propositions.  Accepted-path
   correctness composes only when guard faithfulness, extraction, compilation,
   exact solving and projection all hold.  Fallback identity and declared
   resource checks are engineering invariants; natural-language faithfulness
   and endpoint gain remain empirical.
3. **Prospective TSP confirmation.** On the frozen OPT-Engine size-13--19
   split, the strong static controller scored `54/140` and the typed constraint
   skill `100/140`, with 46 corrections, zero regressions and +32.86 percentage
   points.  Gains were positive at every size; all 40 skill abstentions remained
   wrong in the denominator.
4. **Second-source OR-Debug confirmation.** After development and a passed
   grouped promotion gate, the unchanged typed repair library scored
   `2752/2845` (96.73%) versus `828/2845` (29.10%) for a generic fresh-IIS
   static repair policy: 1,924 corrections, zero regressions and +67.63 points.
   Sixty-five of 100 held-out structural groups improved, 35 tied and none
   declined (`p=2.71e-20` by the prespecified group sign test).  Ninety-three
   ambiguous cases were explicit abstentions and stayed in the denominator.
5. **Compositional preservation under public-only routing.** A single
   fail-closed router processed the frozen 140 TSP and 2,845 OR-Debug public
   confirmation envelopes.  All 2,985 records uniquely selected the expected
   skill, all 2,985 compact outputs exactly matched their frozen direct-family
   outputs, and no route mismatch, abstention or execution failure occurred.
   This is retrospective output-identity evidence, not a new accuracy estimate
   or a learned-router result.
6. **Audited external comparison boundary.** Official-prompt BIND and
   official-code CoE adaptations were locally rerun on the same retrospective
   fixed-40 identities.  BIND scored 31/40 before and 40/40 after answer-bearing
   repair.  CoE's adapted strict scalar rows scored 3/40 and 1/40, but 25/29
   completed outputs fell outside the fixed safe adapter.  OptiTrust has no
   verified runnable official artifact in the inspected arXiv release.  These
   rows document reproducibility and compatibility; unequal information and
   execution contracts prevent an unconditional leaderboard claim.

## Current question and argument

**Research question:** can a fixed model improve final optimization-modeling
accuracy through a reusable, checked inference procedure, and which part of that
procedure actually produces the gain?

The project separates three questions that require different evidence: whether
a harness improves the benchmark endpoint, whether its internal construction is
correct under a formal contract, and whether the useful construction was learned
and transfers. Static selective compilation has a completed positive result.
More recent component experiments have established a restricted construction
mechanism but not its model-acquired use. A matched development study instead
found complementary coverage from two formulation interfaces. Its prespecified
mixed-policy gain did not transfer to 70 further development identities. The
order-only arm improved the endpoint in a secondary comparison; this does not
establish that component synthesis or automatic learning caused the gain.
Subsequent checked finite-domain recovery, using the exact same archived
responses, increased direct accuracy from27/70 to32/70 without regressions.
All five corrections occur in one family. The subsequent frozen135-instance
selection study extends the mechanism gain to two other assigned families:
ordinary41/135 becomes recovery44/135, with3corrections/0regressions. However,
compile-only feedback scores45/135 and has a higher family-macro accuracy.
The prespecified primary recovery-versus-feedback gate fails, so this version
is closed without final confirmation or tuning on those selection cases.
An explicitly post hoc [cascade complementarity audit](DCP_CASCADE_COMPLEMENTARITY_20261014.md)
found that routing to recovery only when feedback has no numerical candidate
would yield 51/135 on the exposed selection cases versus feedback's 45/135.
It may use three model responses instead of two and has no matched third-call
control, so it is a new hypothesis rather than a rescued positive gate or a
paper-ready automatic-method effect. The 219 reserved identities remain closed.

A defensible working framing is **selective trusted compilation for optimization
modeling**. “Verified recursive self-improvement” remains a research hypothesis,
not the demonstrated headline. Renaming the implementation RSI would not supply
missing acquisition, ablation or generalization evidence. No new method name or
submission title is frozen here.

## Method to describe, with attribution kept separate

The input is a public optimization statement. The model proposes a formulation
through a fixed interface; deterministic parsing, compilation and bounded
numerical execution decide whether a candidate can produce an admissible output.
The final benchmark evaluator is separate and cannot select or repair the
candidate. Failed, unsupported, unavailable and preparation-failed identities
remain in the relevant denominator.

The earlier V9 mechanism recognizes a limited set of public-text families,
parses their data, builds audited formulations and preserves the backend output
on abstention. Its contribution is reliable selective construction in a stated
coverage region. It does not establish automatic discovery of that region.

The newer order interface permits a formal Boolean relation over at most five
integer slots. Deterministic search tries the fixed space of at most two signed
comparison terms; an independent checker verifies the resulting identity and a
per-use guard checks its assumptions. Both study conditions receive both
interface descriptions, seven fixed guidance items and three authored formulas.
The difference is the permitted response interface, not privileged knowledge
withheld from the direct control. The four authored mechanism checks yielded
three constructions and one fixed-space failure. They are not benchmark trials
or learned artifacts. See [component development](ORDER_COMPONENT_DEVELOPMENT_RESULTS.md).

For the current matched experiment, generate D1,O1,D2,O2 once each without
feedback. Seal all responses, execute eligible candidates under the same
numerical budget, and select the first numerically validated candidate under
each arm's fixed priority. Seal these selections before native evaluation. Shared
candidates do not create extra observations or free additional attempts. Physical
cost and each arm's attributed cost must be reported separately.

The later recovery mechanism expands only explicitly bounded integer intervals
into the identical finite value set, checks a domain-only witness, and runs the
complete original parser/compiler on the derived proposal. It first preserves
the complete original two-candidate selection before attempting recovery. No
model call or native-score selection is added, but extra local solving is needed.
This is deterministic authored elaboration, not automatic construction learning.

## Theory that the evidence can support

1. **Fallback identity:** the selective wrapper returns the same archived backend
   result whenever its adoption predicate is false. This is an implementation
   identity, not an accuracy guarantee for adopted outputs.
2. **Conditional component preservation:** weak-order enumeration is complete for
   the restricted comparison grammar. If the formal contract is correct and its
   assumptions hold at use, replacing it with the verified expression preserves
   that expression's value. This does not prove natural-language faithfulness,
   constraint completeness or solver performance.
3. **Fixed-priority selection:** the mixed arm retains an eligible D1. It does
   not guarantee non-regression against D1-then-D2 because a correct D2 may be
   lost when D1 is ineligible and O1 is unsuccessful. Eligibility is not native
   correctness. Zero observed regressions on eleven examples is not a theorem.
4. **Promotion control:** the earlier sequential-testing argument requires
   frozen candidates, valid family units and genuinely fresh outcomes. The
   current development comparison does not satisfy or claim that confirmation
   gate. The implementation of a gate is not evidence that its primary test passed.
5. **Exact domain elaboration and original-selection retention:** enumerating
   every integer in a stated finite interval preserves that value set. If all
   remaining relations, objectives and projections are unchanged, identity on
   assignments preserves the specified finite-domain interpretation. It does
   not establish equivalence to a rejected old program's executable semantics
   or source faithfulness. The recovery wrapper also returns every original
   eligible output unchanged, including D2. These conditional properties do not
   establish novel enumeration, solver-time guarantees or cross-family efficacy.

Search from a formal specification, library learning and checked substitution
have substantial prior art. Our restricted implementation alone does not establish
algorithmic novelty; the candidate contribution needs measured optimization-
modeling value beyond simple composition and shared fixed knowledge. The bounded
prior-art audit is in [component synthesis prior art](COMPONENT_SYNTHESIS_PRIOR_ART.md).
