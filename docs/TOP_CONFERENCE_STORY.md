# Benchmark-Grounded Proof-Carrying Knowledge Compilation: current paper story

Updated 2026-10-14. This document is the concise argument map for the current
paper. Historical execution details, rejected variants and immutable closures
remain in PROJECT_HANDOFF.md and the linked experiment artifacts.

## One-sentence argument

Optimization-modeling knowledge can be represented as versioned,
proof-carrying skill programs: benchmark failures motivate typed candidates, a
fixed verifier and compiler enforce declared semantic and resource obligations,
a public-only router deploys one unambiguous skill or abstains, and a frozen
grouped endpoint decides promotion. This methodology improves strong static
controls on prospective TSP and OR-Debug confirmation splits and preserves both
behaviors behind one mixed router.

The current evidence supports benchmark-grounded skill engineering. It does not
support unrestricted RSI, universal semantic correctness, a learned router
under distribution shift or successful automatic skill acquisition.

## Why the problem matters

Language-to-optimization systems can produce executable models with missing,
misbound or unintended relations. Parsing, compilation and successful solving
therefore do not establish that the formulation captures the public problem.
Free-form guidance also makes reusable knowledge hard to version, verify,
route, retire and compare.

The method changes the unit of knowledge from prompt text to a typed skill:

    k = (g, e, t, v, r, p),

where g is an applicability guard, e a typed evidence extractor, t a
transformation, v a semantic verifier, r an exact resource checker and p a
provenance record. The versioned library is

    L_t = (K_t, rho_t, V, E, ledger_t).

The proposer may suggest a skill, but it cannot edit V or E and cannot certify
or promote its own output.

## Contribution stack

### C1. Executable knowledge-acquisition methodology

Finite typed candidate languages, retained counterexamples, trusted
compilation, selective routing and independent promotion define a reproducible
library transition. This is more specific than prompt tuning or asking an agent
to improve itself.

### C2. Conditional reliability framework

The paper gives bounded propositions for:

- finite termination in a declared grammar;
- extensional correctness on an exhaustively enumerated verification domain;
- minimum repair within a frozen finite grammar and distance;
- compliance with declared compiler resource limits;
- accepted-path correctness when guard, extraction, compilation, exact solving
  and projection premises all hold;
- byte-identical fallback outside the accepted region; and
- anytime promotion-error control when each generation uses valid fresh groups
  and a prespecified error budget.

These results are conditional. They do not prove public-language faithfulness,
runtime success, distributional generalization or benchmark accuracy.

### C3. Prospective TSP confirmation

On the frozen OPT-Engine size-13–19 split, the strong static controller scores
54/140 and the typed constraint skill scores 100/140. The skill produces 46
corrections, zero regressions and a 32.86-point absolute gain. Gains are positive
at every size, and all 40 skill abstentions remain incorrect in the denominator.

### C4. Second-source OR-Debug replication

After a passed grouped promotion gate, the frozen typed repair library scores
2752/2845 (96.73%) versus 828/2845 (29.10%) for a generic fresh-IIS static
repair policy. There are 1,924 corrections, zero regressions and a 67.63-point
gain. Sixty-five of 100 held-out structural groups improve, 35 tie and none
decline; the prespecified group sign-test p-value is 2.71e-20. All 93
abstentions remain incorrect.

This is replication of the methodology across two benchmark sources and task
types. It is not transfer of one unchanged skill across sources.

### C5. Frozen-interface composition

A single fail-closed public-only router processes 140 TSP and 2,845 OR-Debug
confirmation envelopes. All 2,985 records route uniquely to the expected
skill, and all 2,985 mixed outputs exactly match their pinned direct-family
outputs. There are zero route mismatches, terminal failures, missing records,
duplicates, invalid rows or unexpected rows.

This establishes retrospective behavior preservation for the frozen public
interfaces. It is not fresh accuracy or learned-router generalization.

### C6. Audited comparison and falsifiable construction boundary

The fixed common-40 panel locally reruns runnable official methods under
disclosed adapters:

- BIND scores 31/40 on its answer-blind first round and 40/40 after official
  answer-bearing feedback. These are different information budgets.
- CoE paper/current snapshots score 3/40 and 1/40 under the fixed safe adapter,
  while 25 and 29 completed outputs respectively lie outside adapter support.
  These rows do not measure unrestricted native CoE.
- The inspected OptiTrust arXiv v1 source contains no verified runnable
  implementation, complete prompts, checkpoint or inference script. No paper
  score substitutes for a local reproduction.
- A separately labeled OptiTrust-style indexed adapter passes a frozen bounded
  transport gate but fails two prospective two-family method gates with zero
  optimal review views and zero consensus cells. It supplies no accuracy row
  and does not rank the unreleased native system.

The first bounded automatic proposer returns a valid member of a frozen
64-candidate count grammar and passes 20,977 semantic assignments, but fails
the exact resource obligation. Exhaustive answer-free verification shows that
all six one-field repairs fail and a joint two-field change is the unique
minimum passing repair. The proposer result is negative; the deterministic
repair is not model discovery.

## Historical anchors and qualification evidence

V9 improves its coupled Text2Opt Base from 200/400 to 259/400, and typed
BinPacking improves from 37/160 to 158/160. These results motivate complete
trusted compilation and selective adoption. They are hand-authored or
controller-authored mechanisms, not evidence of autonomous skill acquisition.

DCP guidance, ordering, feedback, recovery and format studies are
qualification evidence. Their failed gates show that compilation, candidate
availability, local repair and feedback can all improve intermediate metrics
without improving the frozen endpoint. Detailed trajectories belong in
Supplementary Information unless they change the central interpretation.

## Strongest causal gap

The exact count repair exposes an interaction between bound strategy and
zero-tail semantics. The decisive remaining experiment compares:

- flat feedback; and
- interaction-aware separating-witness feedback.

The arms must use the same source candidates, atomic facts, grammar, model,
schema, output ceiling, request budget and verifier. The primary outcome is
verifier pass. Secondary outcomes are minimum-repair hit, response validity,
cost and regression. The prespecified canary gate is at least two net paired
corrections and zero regressions.

Until that comparison passes, the paper may claim verified authored skill
evolution and verifier-mediated finite repair. It may not claim that the model
automatically learns useful skills.

## Positioning against prior methods

The paper's distinction is the complete connection among:

1. benchmark-grounded failure evidence;
2. a typed, finite and versioned knowledge representation;
3. semantic and exact resource proof obligations;
4. public-only selective routing;
5. full-denominator paired endpoint promotion; and
6. an immutable accepted and rejected lineage.

Program synthesis, formal verification, selective prediction, language-to-
optimization agents and risk-controlled testing are established areas. The
contribution is their executable integration and measured behavior on
optimization-modeling benchmarks, not novelty of each individual component.

## Claim ladder

### Supported now

- typed proof-carrying skills improve benchmark endpoint accuracy over strong
  static controls on two frozen confirmation studies;
- the acquisition and promotion methodology replicates across two sources;
- the two frozen family skills compose without changing direct outputs;
- finite grammars admit bounded termination and verifier-relative soundness;
- rejected candidates and minimum repairs can be recorded exactly.

### Supported only conditionally

- compiled-output correctness, assuming faithful guard and extraction,
  semantics-preserving compilation, exact solving and correct projection;
- resource safety for the declared accounted limits;
- promotion-risk control under valid fresh groups and prespecified error
  allocation.

### Not supported

- universal natural-language formulation correctness;
- item-level independence for related templates;
- automatic acquisition of useful skills;
- learned routing under distribution shift;
- superiority over final answer-bearing BIND;
- intrinsic weakness of native CoE;
- official OptiTrust reproduction;
- OptiTrust accuracy or superiority/inferiority from the paper-based indexed preflight;
- unrestricted or weight-level RSI.

## Publication judgment

The current work has a coherent methods-paper core: explicit representation,
bounded theory, prospective multi-source results, composition and reproducible
external comparison boundaries. The main weakness is attribution of knowledge
construction. The successful TSP and OR-Debug skills are authored under the
methodology, while the first model proposer fails.

A positive matched feedback experiment would support a stronger automatic
acquisition claim. A negative result would not invalidate the verified
authored-skill paper, but the manuscript should then present knowledge
construction as human- or search-proposed and mechanically governed.
